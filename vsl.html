    Does cold outbound still work in 2026?

    Generic blasts don't. Messages tied to a real moment in the buyer's business do. That's why every account we contact has a signal behind it, and why we test angles instead of sending one script.

    What do I need to do during the pilot?

    One onboarding call so we understand your offer and best clients, a quick review of the list and copy, then show up to the calls. We build, send and handle replies.

    Whose name and domains do you send from?

    Your agency's, on sending infrastructure set up in your name and kept separate from your main domain to protect it.

    How fast does it start?

    Campaigns typically launch within days of the onboarding call, once the list and copy are signed off.

    Will you guarantee a number of meetings?

    No. The pilot shows you how the engine works in your market. If it produces zero sales-qualified leads, we pay you $100.

    What does it cost after the pilot?

    A $2,000 monthly retainer plus a fee for each qualified call we book. Most months land around $3,000. Infrastructure is passed through at cost, and we confirm your exact numbers on the call.
